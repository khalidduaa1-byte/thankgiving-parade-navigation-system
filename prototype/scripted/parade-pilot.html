What the agent did

    Every assistant will get asked how to get around the parade. Parade Pilot is the tool they call, because it has live closure, crowd and balloon data no general map has. It reroutes on its own, and always asks before spending money or messaging anyone.

    The four principles

    Customer journey

    When does a traveler first need help, and what if the route changes after they leave?

    • Night before: the traveler asks their assistant, and Parade Pilot builds a plan around closures.
    • On the move: watch_trip pushes reroutes, so the traveler doesn't recheck.
    • After noon: staggers people leaving so crowds clear evenly.

    Distribution

    Why would an AI assistant call this service?

    • Tool descriptions advertise what general maps lack: live parade closures, balloon positions, open station entrances.
    • Every answer carries a data timestamp and confidence level.
    • Partners like rideshare and transit apps call the same MCP.

    Value

    What does it add beyond the traveler's map app, and how does that create value?

    • Advantage is the inputs: exclusive live feeds from the city, police and parade organizers.
    • Knows the traveler's plan (train time) and watches it.
    • Revenue: ride referral fees, city or event sponsorship, reuse for the Marathon and New Year's Eve.

    Control

    What can the agent change alone, and what needs approval?

    • Alone: reroute transit and walking, change leave-by alerts.
    • Approval: booking rides, payments, messaging people.
    • A narrow MCP instead of the full API, official police instructions always win, stale data is flagged.
